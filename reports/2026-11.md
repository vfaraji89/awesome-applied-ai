# Applied AI hiring, November 2026 (so far)

*What 795 AI job ads published in November 2026 so far asked for, read from public company career pages and job boards.*

## The month in three numbers

- **795** AI job ads, against 737 the month before
- **76%** ask for software fundamentals, the most asked-for skill
- **20%** are remote; the median ad asks for **5 years** of experience

## Who is being hired

- Applied AI Researcher: 18% of ads (-1 pts)
- AI Platform Engineer: 16% of ads (-4 pts)
- Forward Deployed Engineer: 15% of ads (+3 pts)
- Applied AI Engineer: 12% of ads (+7 pts)
- Agentic AI Engineer: 12% of ads (+1 pts)

## The skills they ask for

- Software fundamentals: 76% (±0 pts)
- Agents and tool use: 64% (+1 pts)
- Customers and adoption: 50% (-1 pts)
- Evals and observability: 45% (-1 pts)
- Models and serving: 45% (-10 pts)
- Product judgement: 30% (-4 pts)

**Biggest moves:** Models and serving -10 pts, Product judgement -4 pts, Security and governance +4 pts.

## What the job is

- Build agents and tool use: 22% of ads that describe the work
- Lead and mentor engineers: 20% of ads that describe the work
- Ship AI features to production: 20% of ads that describe the work
- Prototype and iterate fast: 18% of ads that describe the work
- Integrate with existing systems: 16% of ads that describe the work
