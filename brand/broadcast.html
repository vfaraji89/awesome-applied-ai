The rules all four keep.

  • Choreography: posts, then frame, then label, then number, then the note; 80 to 120ms apart.
  • One easing family: in fast and landing soft; out quicker than in. No bounce.
  • Masks, not fades: edges wipe and words rise out of their line.
  • Numbers land: digits roll or the bead stops, and only then the figure is still.
  • One light sweep at most, once, as the graphic settles.
  • Red means "this one": one headline per group.
  • Plays once when it comes into view; reduced motion shows it at rest.
  • No shadows; depth comes from the wood and the beads.
