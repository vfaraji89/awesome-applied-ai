D6

Visualisations.

How every chart on the site looks: the bars, the colours, the labels.

A · Bead barsrecommended

Each bar is a wooden rod with an ink fill and a bead at its end: the abacus, read as data. Only the headline row is red; numbers in mono, right-aligned.

B · Plain barsquieter

Rounded data ends, one red bar. Reads faster in dense tables, less of the brand.

Category colours, checked for colour blindness

For anything with categories (event types, tool areas, agents). Fixed order, never cycled; red stays first. Passes every check on white; the amber always carries a text label. Today's set fails (ink and warm grey read as grey, the green is dull).

1 rust2 blue3 amber4 rose5 teal6 violet7 green
  • One axis, never two scales in one chart.
  • Thin marks; the grid is a hairline or absent.
  • A legend whenever there are two or more series, plus direct labels on up to four.
  • Text stays ink, grey or muted; colour sits on the mark beside it.
  • Numbers in Geist Mono; words in Vahid Sora.
  • Every chart has a hover tooltip and a table view.
  • Red means "this one": the headline, the live, the today. Never "series 4".
  • No shadows, no 3D, no gradients on data except the bead's own shine.
